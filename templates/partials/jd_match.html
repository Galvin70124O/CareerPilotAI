How the match is worked out{{ icon("chevron-down", 16, class="chevron") }}

Half is word overlap: how much the two texts share the same words, weighted so rare, specific words count more (TF-IDF cosine similarity). Half is the share of the skills the job description names that your {{ noun }} also names; "ReactJS" counts as React. If it names no skill we know, there is no skill match to show, only the word overlap. The half-and-half split is our design choice. {% if source == "resume" %}Skills to work on are the ones you don't have, or only list or studied without a project or job behind them.{% endif %}
